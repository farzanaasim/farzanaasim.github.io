Race for the White Sun

Historical Fiction

The first ice of winter had just begun to crust over the fjord when Roald Amundsen stood on the deck of the Fram, staring into the endless white beyond the horizon. Wind tugged gently at his beard, the cold biting softly at his cheeks. He had always believed he would sail north toward the top of the world—that was the dream that had shaped him, carried him across oceans, taught him hardship and hope.
