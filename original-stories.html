Race for the White Sun

Historical Fiction

In 1911, two expeditions raced toward the South Pole. Roald Amundsen reached it first. Robert Falcon Scott arrived weeks later and never returned home. This story imagines the human side of their journeys: ambition, preparation, courage, disappointment, and loss.
