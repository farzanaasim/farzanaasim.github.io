About Me

I come from a family with deep literary roots. My father, Maajed Siddiqui, was a renowned Urdu and Punjabi poet from Pakistan, and my brother, Yawar Maajed, continues that legacy. Their influence nurtured my love of reading and writing.

My journey later took me into education and research. Living and working in different countries introduced me to different cultures and ways of thinking. Over time, I became increasingly curious about the experiences that shape people’s lives, the choices they make, and the beliefs they carry with them.

Writing gives me a way to explore that curiosity. Sometimes an idea begins with something I have read, sometimes with an experience, and sometimes with a question that stays in my mind long after it first appears.
